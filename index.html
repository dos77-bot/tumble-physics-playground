How to use Tumble

Tumble is a tabletop physics yard. Drop things, knock them over, and feel the weight of every bounce.

Look around

Drag an empty patch of the floor to orbit the camera. Pinch (or scroll) to zoom. You are looking at the yard, not walking inside it.

Grab & throw

Press any loose object and drag — it follows your finger. Let go and it keeps the speed of your hand. A quick flick throws; a slow release just sets it down.

Spawn pieces

Pick a material, then a shape, then hit Drop. New pieces fall into the middle of the yard.

  • Wood — light everyday weight, sticks a little
  • Rubber — light and bouncy
  • Metal — heavy, hits hard
  • Ice — almost no grip, slides forever

Ready-made scenes

Warm-up, Tower, Dominos, Bowling, Pyramid, Newton's Cradle, Wrecking ball, and Ramps. Loading a scene replaces what is on the floor.

The world

Gravity runs from a soft moon pull (~0.16g) to a hard crush (~2.4g). Slow-mo stretches every bounce. Pause freezes the yard. Clear sweeps pieces but keeps the floor. Reset reloads the current scene.

Things that feel good

  • Stack the tower, then tap it with a metal sphere
  • Line up dominos and roll the ball from the end
  • Pull the first cradle ball back and let it click through
  • Stand aside and let the wrecking ball swing into the wall

Works offline once loaded. On Android: open in Chrome → menu → "Add to Home screen" for a full-screen app.
